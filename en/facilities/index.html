Facilities & services

Relax and enjoy

Modern, spacious facilities with everything you need for a sun and beach holiday in Mallorca.

Pool

The pool with a vertical garden

An oasis of freshness surrounded by greenery.

A unique pool with a vertical garden that will surprise you, surrounded by lush vegetation, comfortable loungers and state-of-the-art parasols.

Vertical gardenLoungersParasols

Garden

A garden to lose yourself in

Over 300 m² of garden with tropical plants.

Next to Tonino's Fine Cocktail Rooftop Bar, with loungers for sunbathing while you enjoy your favourite drink, surrounded by tropical greenery that invites you to unwind.

300 m²LoungersTonino's

Sun terrace

The sun terrace

Loungers and Balinese beds overlooking the Mediterranean.

Gaze at the horizon and feel the sea breeze as you sunbathe in one of our lounger and Balinese bed areas.

The perfect place to recharge and rest to the sound of the waves.

Balinese bedsSea views

Hot tubs

Seafront hot tubs

Relax outdoors with views of the Mediterranean.

Our outdoor hot tub area is perfect for anyone looking for an exclusive experience, with everything you need to fully unwind.

OutdoorsSea views

Open from 1 May to 30 September.

Sauna & gym

Sauna and gym

To switch off, or to keep up your routine.

After a day at the beach, the sauna is the best place to relieve stress and relax completely.

The gym is equipped with modern machines and free weights. And if you prefer to train outdoors, there's a large green area for yoga or exercise.

SaunaMachinesFree weights

Cycling

Free Bike Station

A superb area for cycling, and a safe place for your bike.

Store your bike safely and free of charge during your holiday.

We work with Why Not Cycling for bike hire, guided rides and accessory support. Ask us and we'll put together a tailor-made experience.

FreeBike hireGuided rides
